================================================================================
RECRUITSAGE - FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (FRS)
================================================================================

1. CHROME EXTENSION (CLIENT-SIDE)
   1.1 Floating Trigger Button:
       - Injects a floating action button ("RecruitSage Intel") into company 
         details pages on https://recruit.thapar.edu/.
       - Non-intrusive, draggable or docked to the right edge of the viewport.
   1.2 Automated DOM Scraper:
       - On trigger click, extracts:
         * Company Name & Hiring Profile/Role Title
         * Compensation & Breakdown (CTC, Base, Stipend, Bond/Service Agreement)
         * Eligibility Criteria (CGPA cutoff, allowed branches, backlog criteria)
         * Full Job Description (JD) and role requirements
         * Important drive schedules/dates
   1.3 Interactive Side-Panel / Floating Drawer:
       - Displays a slide-over panel on the right side of the active browser tab.
       - Real-time Pipeline Progress Tracker:
         * Stage 1: "Scraping page context..."
         * Stage 2: "Matching Thapar historical question bank..."
         * Stage 3: "Querying Glassdoor & AmbitionBox reviews..."
         * Stage 4: "Analyzing Reddit & developer sentiment..."
         * Stage 5: "Auditing 9-point red flags & CTC traps..."
         * Stage 6: "Synthesizing Placement Dossier..."
   1.4 Structured Dossier Presentation:
       - Renders tabbed/card views:
         * Overview & Fit Scorecard
         * Compensation Reality (In-Hand vs CTC, hidden deductions)
         * Red Flag Alerts (High-visibility warning banners)
         * Campus Historical Intel (Past visit years, shortlist ratios, exact interview questions)
         * Culture & Employee Reviews (Glassdoor/AmbitionBox/Reddit highlights)
         * Alumni Connect (Direct 1-click LinkedIn search links for TIET seniors)
         * 48-Hour Prep Guide (Priority DSA topics, core CS subjects, HR tips)
   1.5 Interactive Doubt-Solving Chat Interface:
       - In-panel conversational chat widget allowing the student to ask free-form 
         follow-up questions (e.g. "Does this company ask Dynamic Programming in OA?", 
         "What is the work-life balance for freshers?", "Should I apply if my CGPA is 7.8?").
       - Streaming response tokens for smooth real-time reading.

2. LOCAL BACKEND & MULTI-AGENT PIPELINE (FASTAPI + PYTHON)
   2.1 Architecture Choice: Option A (Lean Asynchronous Task-Graph):
       - High-speed concurrent Python orchestrator running via asyncio.gather().
       - 4 Lightweight Worker Agents execute in parallel (< 4 seconds total data gathering):
         * Worker 1 (Campus Intel): RapidFuzz + vector matcher on Thapar JSON & CSV.
         * Worker 2 (Reddit & Community Sentiment): Free DuckDuckGo search over r/developersIndia & Blind.
         * Worker 3 (Glassdoor & AmbitionBox): Free DuckDuckGo search over review domains.
         * Worker 4 (Alumni Dork Generator): Generates precision LinkedIn search dorks for TIET seniors.
       - Central Synthesis & Auditor Agent (Ollama: qwen2.5:7b):
         * Consolidates the aggregated context.
         * Audits the 9-point red flags and CTC breakdown.
         * Produces the validated final Dossier in strict JSON format.
         * End-to-end target time: ~10–15 seconds total on M4 Mac.
   2.2 Campus Knowledge & Question Bank Engine:
       - Loads historical campus placement JSON and exported question bank CSV/JSON.
       - Entity Normalization: Maps variations (e.g., "DE Shaw India Software", "D.E. Shaw", 
         "DE Shaw") using semantic embeddings (sentence-transformers / Ollama embeddings) 
         combined with fuzzy matching (RapidFuzz).
       - Context Retrieval: Surfaces exact past questions (DSA, System Design, OS/DBMS, HR) 
         and historical selection statistics for the matched company.
   2.3 Web Research Agents (DuckDuckGo + Targeted Scrapers):
       - Culture & Review Agent:
         * Queries DuckDuckGo for `site:glassdoor.co.in/Reviews "[Company]" "[Role]"` 
           and `site:ambitionbox.com/reviews "[Company]"`.
         * Extracts rating metrics (work-life balance, job security, salary satisfaction).
       - Reddit & Community Sentiment Agent:
         * Queries DuckDuckGo for `site:reddit.com/r/developersIndia "[Company]"`, 
           `site:reddit.com/r/cscareerquestions "[Company]"`, and Blind discussions.
         * Extracts candid fresher feedback: project allocations, work pressure, appraisal reality.
       - Alumni & Network Agent:
         * Formulates smart Google Dorks:
           `site:linkedin.com/in ("Thapar" OR "TIET" OR "Thapar Institute") "[Company]"`
           `site:linkedin.com/in ("Thapar" OR "TIET") "[Company]" "[Role]"`
         * Returns ready-to-click URLs directly to active Thapar alumni profiles.
   2.4 The 17-Point Red-Flag & Corporate Footprint Audit Engine:
       - Evaluates company JD, LinkedIn presence, and web intel against a comprehensive 17-point checklist:
         1. Low LinkedIn Footprint (< 5,000 followers or unverified presence).
         2. Ultra-Lean Team Size (< 10-50 employees, solo fresher risk without senior engineering mentors).
         3. Newly Founded / Early-Stage Startup Runway Risk (founded within last 1-3 yrs with unproven campus pedigree).
         4. Mandatory Unpaid / Low-Stipend Internship & Conditional PPO Baiting.
         5. Document & Marksheet Withholding (demanding original educational certificates / illegal under AICTE).
         6. Service Bonds & Monetary Penalties (1-3 yr bonds, deposit demands).
         7. CTC Inflation & Deferred Phantom Pay (low base, deferred bonuses, 4-yr stock cliffs).
         8. Delayed Joining / Offer Revocation history.
         9. Probation Traps & Aggressive PIP (Performance Improvement Plan) culture.
         10. Role Bait-and-Switch (hired as SDE -> assigned to L1/L2 Support or manual QA).
         11. Predatory Notice Periods (3-6 months for entry-level) & Overly Broad Non-Competes.
         12. Delayed Stipends & Irregular Salary Disbursal History.
         13. Toxic Management & Unpaid Overtime / Weekend shifts.
         14. Mandatory Rotational / Night Shifts (US/UK shifts for freshers).
         15. Mass Layoffs, Hiring Freezes, or Financial Instability.
         16. Shady Corporate Domain & Free Email Contacts (@gmail/@yahoo for official placement drives).
         17. Tech Stack Stagnation (internal proprietary tools, dead-end technologies).
   2.5 Dossier Synthesis Engine:
       - Uses local Ollama model (Qwen 2.5 7B / Llama 3.1 8B) to structure findings 
         into a unified JSON schema.
       - Assigns a calculated "Fit & Risk Score" (e.g., Green Flag / Amber Caution / Red Flag).
   2.6 Conversational RAG & Doubt-Solver:
       - Maintains conversation state in-memory per company session.
       - Uses RAG over the aggregated dossier, JD, campus question bank, and web snippets 
         to answer student queries with precise citations.

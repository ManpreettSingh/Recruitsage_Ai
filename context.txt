================================================================================
RECRUITSAGE - PROJECT CONTEXT & DOMAIN OVERVIEW
================================================================================

1. Background & Problem Statement:
   - Placement season at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET) 
     is intense and fast-paced.
   - Companies open and close applications on short notice on the placement 
     portal (https://recruit.thapar.edu/).
   - Students currently undergo a tedious, manual multi-step research process 
     for every single visiting company:
     a) Cross-checking Glassdoor & AmbitionBox for real ratings and culture.
     b) Digging through Reddit (r/developersIndia, r/cscareerquestions) and Blind 
        for candid employee experiences, delayed onboardings, and PIP culture.
     c) Scrutinizing the Job Description (JD) to spot hidden traps: 2-3 year bonds, 
        monetary penalties, inflated CTC (inflated base vs. phantom variable/stocks), 
        and night shift requirements.
     d) Frantically asking seniors or searching through scattered Google Sheets / 
        JSON drives to find out:
        - Has this company visited Thapar before?
        - How many rounds were conducted?
        - What exact Online Assessment (OA), Technical, and HR questions were asked?
     e) Searching LinkedIn manually to find TIET alumni working in that exact company 
        for referrals and interview advice.

2. Project Vision:
   - Build "RecruitSage": a 100% free, local-first Agentic AI placement intelligence 
     copilot.
   - Operates directly inside the Thapar placement portal (https://recruit.thapar.edu/) 
     via a sleek Chrome Extension side-panel.
   - Automates the extraction of company drive details, matches them against local 
     campus historical placement data and question banks, launches parallel web 
     research agents, conducts a 9-point red-flag audit, and generates a structured, 
     actionable Dossier.
   - Provides an interactive, real-time AI placement counselor (like ChatGPT) where 
     students can chat and clarify any company-specific doubts (e.g., "What OS concepts 
     did they ask last year?", "Is the in-hand salary worth applying for?").

3. Target Environment & Hardware:
   - Host Machine: Apple Silicon M4 MacBook Air, 16 GB Unified Memory, 512 GB SSD.
   - Execution Model: Local-first inference via Ollama (e.g., Qwen 2.5 7B / Llama 3.1 8B) 
     leveraging Apple Metal (MPS) for high tokens/sec and complete privacy.
   - Zero-Cost Architecture: Free scraping + DuckDuckGo search (zero paid API keys required).
   - Target Portal: https://recruit.thapar.edu/ (Authenticated student dashboard).

4. Data Assets:
   - Local Historical Placement Records: JSON dataset containing company names, past visit 
     years, eligibility cutoffs, CTC offered, and selection numbers.
   - Local Campus Question Bank: Google Sheet exported to CSV/JSON format containing exact 
     rounds breakdown, OA questions, technical interview DSA/system design questions, and HR questions.

================================================================================
RECRUITSAGE - END-TO-END USER FLOW
================================================================================

Step 1: Company Discovery on Portal
   - The student logs in to Thapar placement portal (https://recruit.thapar.edu/).
   - Navigates to an active company recruitment page or opening notice.

Step 2: Triggering RecruitSage via Chrome Extension
   - The student clicks the floating "RecruitSage Intel" button on the webpage 
     (or clicks the extension action icon in the Chrome toolbar).
   - The extension immediately opens the sleek side-panel drawer over the right side 
     of the Thapar portal page.

Step 3: Client-Side Page Context Extraction (Scraping)
   - The extension content script extracts the on-page company drive context:
     * Company Name
     * Job Profile / Role
     * CTC Package & Compensation Structure
     * Eligibility Criteria (CGPA, branches, year)
     * Job Description (JD) and scheduled drive dates
   - Sends this structured payload to the local backend:
     `POST http://localhost:8000/api/analyze`

Step 4: Real-time Multi-Agent Research Execution
   - The side-panel displays live visual status indicators as the backend runs:
     [1/6] Extracted page context and drive parameters
     [2/6] Matching Thapar historical question bank & previous visit stats
     [3/6] Querying Glassdoor & AmbitionBox employee ratings & culture
     [4/6] Mining Reddit (r/developersIndia) & Blind community discussions
     [5/6] Executing 17-point Red-Flag and Corporate Footprint audit
     [6/6] Synthesizing final Placement Intelligence Dossier via Ollama / Gemini

Step 5: Structured Dossier Presentation
   - The side-panel renders the comprehensive company dossier:
     * Fit & Risk Verdict (Green / Amber / Red banner)
     * Compensation Breakdown (Base vs Variable vs Bond traps)
     * 17-Point Red-Flag & Corporate Footprint Alerts
     * Thapar Historical Intelligence (Past visits, rounds, exact past questions)
     * Culture & Work-Life Balance Highlights
     * LinkedIn Alumni Connect (1-click direct search links to TIET alumni)
     * 48-Hour Prep Checklist (Key DSA topics, core CS subjects)

Step 6: Interactive Placement Doubt-Solver (ChatGPT-style Chat)
   - At the bottom of the dossier, the student can chat with RecruitSage:
     * "What type of graph/tree questions did they ask in the 2023 technical round?"
     * "Are they strict with the 7.5 CGPA cutoff?"
     * "Is the ₹14 LPA CTC mostly base salary or non-guaranteed bonuses?"
   - The AI streams real-time answers based on the company's full dossier, 
     historical questions, and web intelligence.
